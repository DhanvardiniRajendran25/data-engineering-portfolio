About

Hi, I’m Dhanvardini.

I grew up in Thanjavur, a temple town in Tamil Nadu, and studied computer science at SASTRA University. It was there that I first got obsessed with the question underneath engineering: not just how to move data, but what happens when the data is wrong, late, or never arrives. I wanted to build the infrastructure that answers those questions reliably, at scale, every day.

That conviction took me to Optum, UnitedHealth Group, where I spent two and a half years building the pipelines that healthcare decisions run on. I designed and delivered 10+ ETL pipelines on Azure Databricks processing 4B+ records, cut pipeline failure rates from 40% to 10% through SQL optimization and embedded data quality gates, and productionized 5+ PySpark pipelines from POC handoffs. The work taught me that a pipeline is a promise: if the data is wrong downstream, the whole chain breaks.

I came to Boston for my MS in Information Systems at Northeastern to go deeper on cloud architecture, AI-driven pipelines, and data platform design. Outside the classroom I led a community of 1,500+ builders, directed large-scale hackathons, and mentored 50+ new hires in Optum’s Talent Development Program across data engineering and full-stack tracks. Building people turns out to be the same discipline as building pipelines: clear contracts, reliable handoffs, and no silent failures.

Today I design data systems end to end: bronze-to-gold Medallion pipelines, star-schema warehouses, real-time streaming with Kafka and PySpark, and AI-ready data products on Snowflake and Databricks. I hold AWS Certified Data Engineer and AWS Solutions Architect certifications, and I am looking for a role where the data infrastructure is the product, not an afterthought.
