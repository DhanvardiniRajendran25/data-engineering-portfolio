The Journey

Built on data, from the ground up.

From a small town in Tamil Nadu to building cloud-native data systems in Boston. Every stop a milestone, every milestone a lesson in what makes data reliable at scale.

Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu

Where it started

Born and raised in Thanjavur. Topped school at 95% and served as School Pupil Leader. The town runs on centuries of structure and precision — turns out that is a decent foundation for thinking about data systems.

Data mindset: reliable systems are built on clear contracts and zero tolerance for silent failures.

2018 to 2022 · SASTRA University

The engineering foundation

B.Tech in Computer Science. Got deep into databases, distributed systems, and how data moves across a network. Graduated with four offers — Cognizant, IBM, Deloitte, and Optum — and chose the one where I could build at healthcare scale.

Data mindset: know how the plumbing works before you design the architecture on top of it.

2022 to 2024 · Optum, UnitedHealth Group

Intern to Software Engineer

Joined as an intern, earned a pre-placement offer, and grew into building the pipelines that healthcare decisions run on. Owned data engineering across multiple production systems, mentored 50+ new hires, and was recognized three times for engineering impact.

Data mindset: a pipeline is a promise — if it breaks silently, every decision downstream is wrong.

2024 to 2026 · Northeastern University

Going deeper on data systems

MS in Information Systems, Boston. Went deeper on cloud data architecture, AI-driven pipelines, and data platform design. Led a 1,500+ builder community, ran hackathons for 400+ teams, and earned Northeastern’s top honors including the Laurel & Scroll 100 and the COE Outstanding Graduate Student Award.

Data mindset: architecture decisions made at design time are ten times cheaper than fixes made in production.

Now

Building what’s next

Designing end-to-end data systems — Medallion pipelines, real-time streaming, cloud warehouses, and AI-ready data products — and looking for the team where the data infrastructure is the product, not an afterthought.

Data mindset: the most important pipeline is the one nobody notices because it never fails.
